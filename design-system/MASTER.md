# Design System: Waypoint

*Generated via ui-ux-pro-max guidelines for a budget-transparent AI travel app.*

## Core Aesthetic
- **Avoid:** Generic wanderlust gradients, stock sunset photos, and overused "AI purple-pink" hues.
- **Embrace:** A functional, sleek, and data-rich interface. Tailored, earthy or deeply saturated colors (e.g., deep forest greens, terracotta, or slate blues) with a sophisticated dark mode.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Outfit` (for headings and numbers to give a slightly technical, modern geometry).
- **Secondary Font:** `Roboto Mono` (for data, budgets, and prices).
- *Strict Rule:* No Inter, Arial, or system-default fonts left untouched.

## Color Palette (Example: Slate & Amber)
- **Background:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for dark mode.
- **Surface:** `#1E293B` (Slate 800) for cards.
- **Accent/Interactive:** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500) for sliders and active elements.
- **Text:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for primary, `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) for secondary.

## Component Principles
- **No nested cards:** Cards do not sit inside other cards. Use dividers and typography to separate content.
- **Contrast:** Maintain strict 4.5:1 minimum contrast.
